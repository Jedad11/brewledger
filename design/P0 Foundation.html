Foundation, navigation shell, responsive rules

Design tokens layered on the Brewledger design system, the two navigation shells, and the rules that every screen downstream inherits. No product screens yet.

01Semantic colour

The design system's four-tier green stays as-is for brand and surface. These roles are added on top so a number's meaning is legible before it is read.

Revenue--c-revenue · #14875a
Cost--c-cost · bronze
Profit--c-profit · #0b6b47
Warning--c-warning · #e0a728
Unknown--c-unknown · black 38%

Cost is a muted bronze derived from the system's gold hue, not red — a cost is not a fault. Red stays reserved for cancellation and refund. Gold is not used in this product; there is no rewards tier here.

02Order status — 8 states

One colour and one Thai label per status, used identically in the console inbox, the order detail screen, and the customer tracking screen.

รอชำระเงินรับออเดอร์แล้วกำลังทำพร้อมรับรับแล้วยกเลิกคืนเงินแล้วหมดเวลา

พร้อมรับ is the only filled badge — it is the one status that needs to be spotted across a counter. The three terminal-neutral statuses share one grey so the eye skips them.

03Thai typography

Thai has no inter-word spaces and stacks marks two levels above and below the baseline. Body line-height is 1.5, headings 1.35 — looser than the design system's English-first defaults. Noto Sans Thai carries the Thai glyphs; Manrope remains the Latin and numeral face.

09Design system components, live

Rendered from the Brewledger bundle — the console and customer surfaces compose from these rather than restyled markup.

Two rules that do not bend

Customer web never shows cost data. No cost, profit, margin, expense, stock level, or store-wide total appears on any customer screen — including its loading and error states. The customer is a stranger holding a public link.
